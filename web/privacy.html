Get Down Text TractorPrivacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app does with your screen

Get Down Text Tractor uses Android's Accessibility Service API to show a floating bubble and, only when you tap the bubble or the Quick Settings tile, to read the text that is visible on your screen and take a screenshot so it can read text inside images. Recognition runs entirely on your device. Screenshots are kept in memory only, deleted as soon as the text is read, and never saved or uploaded. Password fields are never read. The app doesn't use accessibility to tap, type or control your phone.

Text you copy

Text you copy is stored only in the app's local history on your phone. You can delete single items or clear all history in Settings. It is never sent to our servers.

What we collect

  • A random device ID, phone model, Android version and app version.
  • If you create an account: your email address (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Anonymous usage counts: when a capture or copy happens, how many characters it had, and which app it came from (its package name). Never the text itself.

We use this to count active users, keep the service working and improve it. We don't sell data or show ads.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all local data. To delete your account and usage records, email contact@getdownaf.info.
